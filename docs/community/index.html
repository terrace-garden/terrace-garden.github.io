Our shared garden

A kind place to return.

Private invitations, shared attention, and room for everyone to feel comfortable.

Keep shared words kind

Use appropriate names and garden goals. Harassment, hateful or sexual content, threats, spam and impersonation are not welcome. Terrace filters obvious objectionable terms before saving shared text.

Report, block or leave

Open Together → Garden safety to report a garden name, goal or gardener. Choose the reason that fits. Reports include only the reported shared text and membership identifiers. Blocking hides that gardener’s name and activity in your own view. You can unblock them later. Leaving keeps your personal plants safe.

Host your space

Hosts can remove a membership. Removing it turns off the current invitation, so the old invitation cannot be used to rejoin. Share a new one only with people you want here. Another person’s personal garden and local focus session remain theirs.

Review and contact

Reports are stored privately for the Terrace operator to review. Access may be removed and invitations revoked for abuse. For a concern needing context, contact veil@tryrlay.com. Never send access keys, invitations or recovery files. Terrace is not an emergency service.
