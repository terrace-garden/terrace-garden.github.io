Private study stays on your device. Together is optional. Only the details needed to grow a shared garden reach its service.

Your own space

Your private focus intention and app choices stay on your device. Garden Passport is optional: your personal plants and arrangements can sync as encrypted data. Only devices holding your private recovery file can open that garden. The service never receives its encryption key. Keep recovery files private; they contain the key and an encrypted garden copy. Terrace does not capture your screen or read your documents or agent conversations.

Optional app protection

On Mac, Terrace keeps local records of apps it hides so it can restore them safely. On iPhone and iPad, optional Screen Time access lets Apple shield the apps, categories, and websites you select. Those private selection tokens stay on your device. Session timing and a random session identifier let its on-device extensions release protection when focus ends. Distraction attempts are not sent to the garden service or announced to friends.

When you grow together

Together is optional. When you create or join a shared garden, its service receives your chosen display name, the garden name and shared goal, a random membership identifier, and session identifiers. Planned minutes, focus status, earned minutes, and timestamps let the group build its world and prevent duplicate rewards. A study table shares its planned start and finish times and who joins it. Your private focus intention and app choices are not sent.

Removing the cloud copy

Choose Delete Passport cloud copy in Garden Passport settings to remove the live encrypted cloud garden. Your local plants and recovery files remain. A random revocation marker without ciphertext prevents old devices recreating it. Create a new Passport to connect devices again. Hosting infrastructure may retain its own backups under its retention policy.

A kind shared garden

Together offers reports, personal blocking and host removal. Reports store the selected reason, reported shared text and random garden/member identifiers for operator review. They never include private intentions, app choices or recovery keys. Report and block records tied to a membership are removed with its shared-data deletion. Community guidelines explain these controls.

A private invitation

An invitation lets someone join your garden. Keep it with the people you want there. Each membership has its own secret key, saved in Apple Keychain on that device. The service stores hashes of membership keys and invitations. The host can replace or turn off the invitation; existing members keep their access.

Leaving or deleting shared data

Leaving revokes this device’s membership and keeps its name and contributions in the group history. Choose Delete my shared data in Together to remove this membership, name and individual session records instead. Anonymous earned totals preserve the remaining group’s world. Hosting passes to a remaining member and the old invitation turns off. If no active members remain, the shared garden is deleted. This leaves your personal garden and separate Garden Passport untouched. Joining from another device creates a separate membership.

Keeping the connection safe

The service uses hashed network-address keys for short-term rate protection. Old rate-limit entries are removed opportunistically. Hosting infrastructure may keep its own connection logs.

Quiet by design

You do not need an email address or an account login. Shared gardens have no chat feed and send no progress notifications. Live garden activity refreshes only when Together is open between focus sessions. Session updates can still be sent or retried quietly so completed work reaches your group.

Help pages and support

Public help pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection metadata under its privacy policy. If you email support, your email provider and Terrace’s support provider process the message so the operator can respond. Include only the details needed to help; never send passwords, garden invitations, membership keys or recovery files.

No advertising or analytics

This version of Terrace has no advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics integration. Terrace does not add advertising or analytics scripts to this page. The hosting provider may use security checks, cookies, and connection logs to protect the service.
