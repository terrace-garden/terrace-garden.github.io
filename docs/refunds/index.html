Kaden Zheng, trading as Terrace, supplies the standalone Mac app. Paddle will handle website sales as merchant of record.

Cancel a renewal

Manage subscription in the Mac app opens Paddle’s secure customer portal. You can also use the link in your Paddle receipt, including when your private Pro restore key is unavailable. Cancel there before the next renewal to stop future charges. A cancellation scheduled for the end of a paid period keeps Pro through that period; the portal will show its effective date. Cancellation does not erase earned garden progress.

Trial choices

If you explicitly choose an eligible 14-day free trial, review its renewal amount and end date before confirming. A payment method is required. The optional trial is limited to one use per private Pro account; this is not identity verification per person. Cancel before the trial converts if you do not want the first recurring charge. A free core edition remains available without starting a trial.

Recovery and offline access

Paid-renewal grace is available for up to 16 days after the last verified paid-through date. A trial that never converted to paid is not eligible for paid-renewal grace. An offline signed paid lease can remain valid for up to 72 hours within its access deadline, so a refund or deactivation may not remove offline access immediately. Reconnect and use Refresh Pro to check current status. Your earned garden remains intact.

Request help with a payment

Use the link in your Paddle receipt or visit Paddle buyer support for payment, cancellation and refund requests. For a technical issue, contact veil@tryrlay.com with your app version and order reference so we can help resolve it. Do not send card numbers, passwords, activation secrets or garden recovery files. Refund requests are assessed under Paddle’s refund policy, its buyer terms and your mandatory consumer rights. Terrace adds no separate refund guarantee.

App Store purchases

Apple manages App Store subscriptions and refund requests separately. The Paddle portal cannot manage Apple purchases. Use Apple’s subscription settings or refund process for those purchases.
