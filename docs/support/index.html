Availability & requirements

Terrace is not publicly available yet. The planned website download is for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs are not included in this first download. Check Apple menu → About This Mac for your chip and macOS version. The iPhone edition is separate; this website does not provide an iPhone installation.

Installing & updating

When the signed Mac download is available, use only the download linked from this website and follow its installation notes. Normal updates preserve your local garden. Between sessions, quit Terrace and replace the app in the same location; do not delete its saved data or reset its settings. Keep a private Garden Passport recovery copy before updating. Automatic updates are not available yet; future versions and their notes will be linked here. The download will be signed and Apple-notarized. You should not need to disable Gatekeeper or change Mac security settings to install it.

Keeping your progress

Updating Terrace preserves its saved garden. Keep using the same installed app identity. Garden Passport in Settings lets you save a private recovery file and connect your earned garden on another device. A live focus session stays on its original device. Keep recovery files private: they contain your garden key.

App protection

In the standalone Mac download, optional protection hides selected apps while their work continues. It does not close or suspend them. The Mac App Store edition supports focus sessions and gardens, but macOS can limit app hiding there. Choose the standalone download for Mac app protection. macOS controls Space animations and system prompts. On iPhone and iPad, protection is optional and uses Apple’s Screen Time permission. If permission is unavailable, your focus timer and garden remain available. Finish the session or use Terrace’s deliberate early-exit flow to release protection.

Shared gardens

A garden invitation is private. Ask its host for a new invitation if the old one has expired or been replaced. Shared updates can wait safely on your device while offline. Reopen Together when connected to retry. Leaving revokes access and keeps shared history. To erase this membership’s name and individual session records, choose Delete my shared data in Together. Your personal garden remains on your device. Anonymous earned totals keep your friends’ world intact; a garden with no active members is deleted.

Local timer sharing

This Mac setting is optional and off by default. Open Settings → Local focus observations → Share timer facts locally only if you want to pair a diagnostic tool. Wait for Published locally, then pair the exact displayed path in that tool, with its own sharing permission. The export contains timer and break facts, never your intention, app choices or garden. It does not judge whether your work succeeded. Turn the switch off to stop sharing, or choose Delete local observations to clear its history. Check the status: an unsuccessful withdrawal cannot confirm that a paired tool has received it. Refresh the paired tool to let it observe the withdrawal; offline copies are not erased immediately. Sharing again requires a new pairing. Your garden and focus session stay intact.

Subscriptions

Website Pro checkout is not available yet. Base pricing is $3.99/month or $29.99/year in USD, with an optional 14-day trial for eligible subscribers. Checkout shows the final total, applicable tax and any available local currency. Website Pro and App Store subscriptions are separate; an App Store purchase is not currently a license for the website download. Website Pro does not unlock the iPhone or App Store editions. Terrace Pro adds three worlds, Pip story collections and guided study-table templates. Core focus, custom durations, the original five worlds, arrangement, progress, recovery and basic shared study stay free. In the App Store editions, one active subscription works on Mac and iPhone with the same Apple Account. Open Settings → Explore Terrace Pro to view the actual price, billing period and any eligible introductory trial before subscribing. Use Restore purchases there after reinstalling or on another device. Active subscribers can choose Manage subscription to open Apple’s controls. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues through the paid period. Failed-payment recovery follows Apple’s configured grace period. Your earned garden and a running focus session are preserved when Pro ends. Apple’s standard license agreement applies to the App Store editions.

Website Pro & recovery

Website Pro checkout is not available yet. In the standalone Mac download, open Settings → Explore Terrace Pro. After completing Paddle checkout, return to Terrace and choose Refresh Pro if access has not appeared yet. Choose Show my restore key and keep it somewhere private. On another Mac, open Have a restore key?, enter the Private Pro restore key and choose Restore Pro. Up to five active device leases can use a private Pro account. To free this Mac’s slot, save the key first, then choose Deactivate this Mac. Deactivation leaves your subscription and garden intact. This key is separate from Garden Passport; it cannot recover a garden, and a Passport recovery file cannot restore Pro. Do not send either secret to support. If a checkout remains pending or a key is lost, contact support; the app will not retry an ambiguous charge or infer ownership from an email alone. For cancellation or payment help without your key, use the link in your Paddle receipt or Paddle buyer support.

Payment recovery & offline access

The website subscription policy provides up to 16 days of paid-renewal grace after the last verified paid-through date while payment is being recovered. The deadline does not keep moving forward on repeated failures. A trial that never converted to a paid subscription does not receive paid-renewal grace. The optional 14-day free trial is limited to one use per private Pro account; this is not a claim of identity-based eligibility per person. A cached signed paid lease can support up to 72 hours offline, within its access deadline. An offline Mac may retain access after a refund or deactivation until it checks successfully or the lease expires. Reconnect and choose Refresh Pro to check current access. None of these events erase earned garden progress or interrupt a running focus session. Website billing and Apple’s configured App Store grace rules are separate. See the website terms and refunds & cancellation policy. Public downloads and live checkout are not available yet.

Why does checkout mention Relay?

Terrace’s website payments use the existing Paddle merchant account named Relay, with its owner’s authorization. You’re buying Terrace Pro for the standalone Mac app, supplied by Kaden Zheng, trading as Terrace. Paddle handles the sale as merchant of record.

Get help

Email veil@tryrlay.com with your Terrace version, device model, operating-system version, and a short description of what happened. For TestFlight builds, you can also use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Never include private Pro restore keys, garden invitations, membership keys, recovery files, or passwords. If you have a concern about shared content or retained data, describe the concern without sharing private access keys.
